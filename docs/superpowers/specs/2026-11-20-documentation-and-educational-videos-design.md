# SysNDD documentation refresh and educational videos: design

Date: 2026-11-20. Status: draft for approval.
Related: sysndd-manuscript issues #49 (curation answer to the AJHG reviewers) and #62
(curator training), the ERN-ITHACA / ESHG Young trainee-curation proposal
(Sept-Oct 2026).

## Intent

The SysNDD documentation has aged. Its user-facing part is thin and it misses whole
tasks, while the developer and deployment chapters make up most of the book. At the
same time SysNDD needs more curators: a small senior team carries almost all curation,
and ERN-ITHACA with ESHG Young wants a training programme for trainee curators. Short,
instructive videos plus a rewritten, task-based documentation should:

1. show clinicians and researchers what SysNDD can do and how to use it;
2. teach new curators to research a gene, read a paper for curation, and enter and
   grade an entity under the current criteria;
3. give the ITHACA programme its webinar and quiz material.

Success: a new reviewer can go from "never used SysNDD" to a correct first entity
using only the videos and the docs, and the AJHG response can point to the programme.

## Goals

- A new repository `../sysndd-educational` holding the video pipeline, scripts and
  build outputs (release assets, not committed).
- Two video tracks: **Use SysNDD** (public features, captured from the live site) and
  **Curate with SysNDD** (curation, recorded on a local development instance, plus
  real PubMed and PMC pages for the research part).
- A worked curation example on a gene not yet in SysNDD: ***MAU2*** (video B4 only).
  Every other video uses a different gene, so viewers see the range of the catalogue.
- The documentation rewritten **in place** in `../sysndd/documentation` (decision A):
  task-based user chapters, each embedding its video; developer material grouped
  separately; screenshots regenerated by the existing tooling.
- The visual system for cards and frames drafted in **Claude Design**, then synced into
  the repository.

## Non-goals

- No change to SysNDD's curation criteria. Videos and docs teach the current rules
  (`documentation/05-curation-criteria.qmd`, `06-re-review-instructions.qmd`) and
  flag gaps for a later, separate decision.
- No app feature work. Video needs that touch the app (e.g. a missing "propose a new
  gene" path) become issues.
- No full LMS, certificates or award tracking. The ITHACA programme owns those; we
  supply videos and question material.
- No translation in the first round (English only).

## Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")

- Recording curation on production, or with real accounts or credentials.
- Teaching a classification the criteria do not support. The *MAU2* category is
  decided by a senior curator, not by the script.
- Overclaiming: an evidence category is not a diagnosis; a functional cluster is a
  hypothesis; ClinVar shown on a gene page is not SysNDD curation.
- Marketing tone, decorative visuals, or a second visual world beside the site.
- Videos that go stale silently. Every video records the site version it shows.

## Constraints

- **Capture sources.** Public videos use https://sysndd.dbmr.unibe.ch. Curation
  videos use the local Playwright stack (`make playwright-stack`, seeded test users,
  `http://localhost`), never production. The stack is CI-only fixture data, so the
  *MAU2* references and ontology terms must exist in it or be seeded for the
  recording.
- **Roles.** Create Entity and approval are Curator+; Reviewers re-review assigned
  entities (Table S2 of the manuscript; `06-re-review-instructions.qmd`).
- **Third-party pages.** PubMed and PMC are filmed as public pages. The *MAU2*
  *Nature Communications* 2026 paper (PMID 41912533) is open access on PMC. Confirm
  its licence (CC BY) before showing figures; text excerpts stay short.
- **Visual identity.** Follow `documentation/10-visual-design-guide.md`: compact,
  table-first, quiet; medical blue `#0d47a1`/`#1e88e5`, teal `#00897b`, status
  colours. Light theme only.
- **Voice.** A synthetic narrator voice (VoiceStudio clone profile, not a real
  person), as in the kidney-genetics videos.
- **Time.** The AJHG response (due 2026-12-16) needs only the commitment and plan,
  not finished videos.

## Approaches considered

1. **Copy the kidney-genetics pipeline as is.** Stills with highlights, narration and
   cards. Proven and quick, but a curation how-to told in stills feels like a slide
   deck.
2. **Fully recorded walkthroughs.** Continuous screen recordings with narration.
   Engaging, but long takes are fragile and costly to re-shoot after UI changes.
3. **Hybrid (chosen).** Stills plus highlights for the overview videos; short
   recorded clips for hands-on steps (searching PubMed, reading the paper, filling in
   the Create Entity wizard). Both styles come from one pipeline
   (`../kidney-genetics-db-educational` already mixes clips into scene files). Short
   clips are cheap to redo.

Picked: 3, by B. Popp, 2026-11-20. Documentation location A (stay in the app repo),
by B. Popp, 2026-11-20.

## The video series

Common form (from the kidney-genetics style guide): 2-4 minutes, one idea per video,
cover card → intro card (the question it answers, 3-4 parts) → screens, clips and
concept cards → "Remember" card (three points) → end card (logo, URL, licence).
Each video states its learning objective as "after this you can…", and each video
stands alone.

### Track A: Use SysNDD (live site)

| # | Title | After this you can… | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | SysNDD in three minutes | explain the entity (gene + inheritance + disease) and the four categories, and say what SysNDD is for | *FBXO11*: AD Definitive, AR Refuted |
| A2 | Look up a gene | read a gene page (entities, constraint, ClinVar, protein view, model organisms) and an entity page (synopsis, phenotypes, variants, publications, history) | *FBXO11*, *ARID1B* |
| A3 | Build a gene panel | filter by category and inheritance, export, cite the release | Definitive, all inheritance |
| A4 | Search by phenotype | combine HPO categories with AND/OR and read the result | ID + seizures + microcephaly |
| A5 | SysNDD for software and AI | query the API and the MCP interface | one gene by API |

### Track C: Analyses and NDDScore (live site)

Each analysis video ends with what the view can and cannot tell you: clusters and
correlations are hypotheses about disorder-level annotations, not patient-level
findings.

| # | Title | After this you can… | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Comparing curation resources | read the overlap and similarity views of `/CurationComparisons`, and explain why absence elsewhere is not disagreement | SysNDD vs PanelApp, Gene2Phenotype, SFARI |
| C2 | Phenotype patterns | use `/PhenotypeCorrelations` (counts, correlogram, phenotype clusters) and `/VariantCorrelations`, and read a correlation correctly | mitochondrion with musculature; frameshift with truncation |
| C3 | Gene networks and functional clusters | build and read `/GeneNetworks`, open a cluster's enrichment and summary, and treat a cluster label as a hypothesis | the chromatin cluster (*ARID1B*, *CHD7*, *KMT2D*) |
| C4 | Phenotypes meet molecules | read `/PhenotypeFunctionalCorrelation`, which shows where phenotype clusters and functional clusters overlap | the metabolic/mitochondrial coupling |
| C5 | How the catalog grows | use `/EntriesOverTime`, `/PublicationsNDD` and `/PubtatorNDD` to follow growth and find literature not yet curated | candidates from PubTator |
| C6 | NDDScore explained | say what NDDScore predicts and from which inputs, read a gene's score and its model card (`/NDDScore`, `ModelCard`), and know its limits: a prioritization aid, not evidence for a category | one curated gene and one uncurated candidate |

**NDDScore timing.** NDDScore is described in a separate manuscript not yet
published (the AJHG editors asked that the SysNDD paper not report it). C6 states
only what the live site and model card already say, cites the NDDScore paper or
preprint once one exists, and is released no earlier than that. The script is
checked by the NDDScore authors.

### Track B: Curate with SysNDD (local instance + PubMed/PMC)

| # | Title | After this you can… | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | How SysNDD grades evidence | apply the criteria: count cases and families, strong and moderate criteria, negative criteria | worked cards: Definitive by count (*ANKRD11*), dual inheritance with AD Definitive and AR Moderate (*SETD1B*), Limited, Refuted (*PGA5*) |
| B2 | Researching a gene | search PubMed and OMIM, find case reports and cohorts, follow a preprint to its publication, check GeneReviews | *TBL1XR1*: a gene whose evidence grew until it was promoted from Limited to Definitive in August 2026, with two disorders (ID and Pierpont syndrome) |
| B3 | Reading a paper for curation | extract what the entity needs: individuals and families, inheritance and *de novo* status, variant types, segregation, functional data, phenotypes in ≥20% | *DDX3X*: Snijders Blok et al. 2015, *AJHG* (PMID 26235985, open access, PMC4573244); X-linked, female predominance |
| B4 | Curating a new gene | create the entity, choose disease ID and inheritance, decide the category from B1, write the synopsis with counts, add phenotypes, variant types and publications, submit | *MAU2* in the Create Entity wizard |
| B5 | Re-reviewing an entry | work an assigned batch: update review and status, submit, refuse to a specialist | a legacy Limited entity in the fixture data |
| B6 | Approving as a curator | approve or edit reviews and statuses, and spot what to send back | the B4/B5 submissions |

The ITHACA webinar is B1-B5 in sequence. Each Track B video ships three or four
multiple-choice questions written from its "Remember" card, which together form the
proposed 10-question check.

**After recording**, a senior curator curates *MAU2* for real on production, under
their own judgement. If the result differs from the recording, the recording is
re-cut. B4 may end with the live entry.

## Documentation refresh (in `../sysndd/documentation`)

Proposed book structure; existing chapters are kept and rewritten where marked:

- **Getting started**: what SysNDD is (01, rewritten around the entity concept,
  embeds A1).
- **Using SysNDD**: one task chapter per Track A video, replacing the long
  page-by-page `02-web-tool.qmd`; API and MCP (03, refreshed, embeds A5); analyses (embeds C1-C5); **new** NDDScore chapter (embeds C6), written from the model card, with the same timing rule as C6.
- **Curating**: evidence criteria (05, restructured with worked examples, embeds B1);
  **new** "Researching a gene and reading a paper" (B2, B3); re-review for
  Reviewers (06, refreshed, B5).
- **Curator guide (new, required)**. Today the user docs cover only the Reviewer's
  re-review. No user-facing page explains the Curator+ workflows, which appear only
  as implementation notes in the deployment chapter. One chapter per workflow, each
  with purpose, who may use it, step-by-step screenshots and pitfalls:
  - Create Entity: identifier, evidence, phenotype/variation, classification, final
    review, draft recovery (B4).
  - Modify Entity: edit review and status, rename disease, deactivate or replace a
    duplicate.
  - Approve Reviews and Approve Statuses: inspect, edit, approve, dismiss,
    batch-approve, duplicate awareness (B6).
  - Manage Re-review: build batches, assign, reassign, monitor, recover refused
    records.
  - Approve Users: assess applications and set roles.
  - GeneReviews coverage, and the Variation suggestions queue.

  The facts come from the app (`app/src/views/curate/` and the authenticated pages
  listed in Table S2 of the manuscript), checked against the local instance during
  capture.
- **Reference**: data model (04, expanded from 240 words: entity, review, status,
  history, categories, ontologies); release and citation; glossary.
- **For developers**: development and deployment (08, 09) unchanged in content,
  moved to their own part.
- The videos page (07) becomes a gallery. Each video also appears in its chapter.

Screenshots in the docs come from the existing `make docs-screenshots`. Facts in the
docs and in the scene files share one source, the criteria and re-review chapters, so
a rule changed in one place is changed in the other.

## Pipeline (in `../sysndd-educational`)

- Start from a copy of `../kidney-genetics-db-educational`: `screencast/` (annotate,
  voice, frames, build_video, clip and capture helpers), `templates/`, `tests/`,
  `justfile`, the scene-file schema with its German keys (kept for compatibility).
  Rebrand: SysNDD logo, colours and type. Replace the tier stripe with the SysNDD
  category colours.
- Capture: Playwright scripts per video. Track A against the live site; Track B
  against the local stack with seeded test users (Curator and Reviewer). PubMed and
  PMC are captured live.
- Narration: VoiceStudio with a new "SysNDD narrator" profile. Pronunciation table for
  SysNDD, VariO, HPO, MONDO, and gene symbols spelled out (*MAU2* "M A U two").
- Output: mp4 with burned-in subtitles plus `.srt`, published as release assets and
  embedded in the docs.
- Each scene file records the site version and capture date. The docs gallery shows
  them.

## Visual design

Claude Design drafts the frame system before any scene is built: cover, intro,
concept, "Remember" and end cards; the screen frame with header, part tracker and
sources band; the highlight style. Inputs are the SysNDD visual guide and live-site
screenshots. The result is pulled into `templates/` through the `/design-sync`
workflow (started by B. Popp) and then held by layout tests, as in the kidney-genetics
repository.

## The legacy reviewer video

The only existing video, "SysNDD Reviewer Tutorial" (YouTube channel @sysndd, video
kRDhtepStbs, embedded in `07-tutorial-videos.qmd`), dates from January 2023. It is
5:49 at 1080p, a screen recording of the re-review flow on the 2023 interface with a
Reviewer test account, using *ASPA* / Canavan disease as the example. A coauthor cut
it; no project files survive. Kept from the source package
(`ReviewerTutorial.zip`): the final mp4, the subtitle text and the script
(`SysNDD_ReviewerVideoTutorial_text_Jan23_CZ_BP.docx`, by C. Zweier and B. Popp).

- The script's content is still valid and becomes the factual base of B5: synopsis
  contents, the ≥20% phenotype rule, minimum variation terms, the PMID format,
  GeneReviews PMIDs, and that changes are visible only after curator approval.
- The interface it shows is outdated. The video stays online, labelled "recorded on
  the 2023 interface", until B5 replaces it.
- The script and subtitles go into `sysndd-educational/legacy/`. The 135 MB mp4 is
  not committed; it is kept as a release asset.
